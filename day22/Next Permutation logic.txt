Step 1️⃣ Find the pivot

From right to left, find the first index i such that:

nums[i] < nums[i + 1]


If not found → array is descending → reverse whole array.

Step 2️⃣ Find the next greater element

From right to left, find index j such that:

nums[j] > nums[i]

Step 3️⃣ Swap nums[i] and nums[j]
Step 4️⃣ Reverse the suffix (i+1 to end)

This makes the permutation just slightly bigger.
